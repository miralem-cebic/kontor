## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Small business status (#36, part of the epic #30): the overview counts the turnover against the limits as § 19 Abs. 2 UStG defines the total turnover, the net part of the payments on the day the money arrived, without services that are not taxable in Germany (before, the net of the invoices of the year). It finds the day on which the 100,000 € of the year are crossed and warns a small business from 80 % of the limit, when it is crossed (with the day, from which on invoices need VAT) and when the previous year was above 25,000 €. Issuing refuses "no VAT, § 19" for an invoice dated after that day, or in the whole year after a previous year above 25,000 €, and the invoice form lists the reason; an older invoice, an invoice dated on the crossing day and a service not taxable in Germany stay possible. The setup assistant and the tax settings explain the waiver of the scheme (§ 19 Abs. 3 UStG: irrevocable, binding for five calendar years, declared to the tax office) before it is chosen. A banner on the overview points out the duty under § 13b UStG (and the advance return, § 18 Abs. 4a UStG) when a small business has an expense with reverse charge or from outside the EU. A tax rate stays rejected for a small business also on small amount invoices, now with a test. Only the payments of the open company are counted; payouts and other companies of the same person are not included yet. The points that are not verified are listed in the source and in the roadmap. No format change.
- Payouts (#31, part of the epic #30), a new area "Auszahlungen": income that arrives without an invoice of kontor, such as the payouts of Apple, Google, Stripe or PayPal. A payout has the day the money arrived, the account, the amount, the payer (a customer from the contacts, so one Apple or Google entity can be reused), the month it covers, the tax treatment, an optional https link to the statement and a note. The tax treatment is chosen by the person and never guessed or preselected: German payer with VAT inside (only the net part is income), business in the EU not taxable (§ 3a Abs. 2 UStG; the payer needs a plausible VAT ID of another EU state), business outside the EU not taxable, small business without VAT, or the platform collects the VAT (a manual treatment with a warning, because how it is treated is an open question). Each choice is explained in one sentence that says to check the document. A small business cannot pick the treatment that has VAT inside, and a company with regular VAT not the small business one. A payout counts as income on the day it arrives, in the overview and in the reports (income per payer and month, year against year before), and it is listed in the customer account of the payer without changing the balance. It is a file of its own (`payouts/`), edited like an expense and removed by soft delete; booking, changing and removing are written to the audit log (`bookPayout`, `changePayout`, `deletePayout`). Payouts are in the daily backup, the CSV and Excel export of the list and the ZIP export. The statement is only a link for now, the payouts are not yet part of the turnover compared with the small business limits, and the UStVA fields for payouts follow with #34.
- Footer from the master data (F26): in the setup assistant (step "Rechnungen") and in the invoice settings, the company chooses which parts of its master data the footer of quotes, invoices, cancellations and dunning letters shows (name and address, phone, email), and a preview shows the footer as it will print, from what is typed so far. The tax number or VAT ID and the bank details always stand. The free text "Fußzeile" is for additions only. Existing companies keep showing every part.
- Customer email and phone on the document (F31): the PDF of quotes, invoices, cancellations and dunning letters prints the email and phone of the customer in the information block, below the customer number, as "Ihre E-Mail" and "Ihr Telefon" ("Your email", "Your phone"). They are not part of the address field, so the address stays in the window of the envelope. A document that was issued keeps printing what its snapshot of the customer held; a customer without email or phone prints nothing. No format change.
- Summary report filling aid (R8, `Zusammenfassende Meldung`, ZM; § 18a UStG), area "Steuern" below the advance return: for a quarter or month, one line per recipient VAT ID with the country, the kind (service to a business in another EU state, or intra-community supply) and the sum of the bases from the issued invoices, with a copy button per value (country, VAT ID without the country code, amount in whole euros), a CSV file and a link to the BZSt online portal. Services count in the period of the service, supplies in the period of the invoice (at the latest the month after), cancellations negative; a small business gets the note that no report is needed. The due date (25th day, no permanent extension) and the 50,000 € limit for monthly reports of goods come from the tax parameters. The VAT IDs of the recipients are checked, and the total of the services is compared with field 21 of the advance return. It is a filling aid, not tax advice; an import file for the portal is not written (open, see the roadmap).
- Reference scenarios for the VAT cases (#37): worked examples with exact cents for the advance return and the summary report in `tests/fixtures/vat`, each with its paragraph and URL, and a property test that net plus tax is the gross amount for any rate and amount. Scenarios that need payouts or received reverse-charge expenses are listed as pending tests.
- UStVA filling aid (R1), area "Steuern": the advance VAT return of a month or quarter for a company with regular VAT, from the issued invoices, their payments and the expenses. Fields 81 and 86 (base and tax at 19 % and 7 %), 41 (intra-community supplies), 21, 60, 45 (reverse charge and supplies not taxable in Germany), 66 (input tax) and 83 (remaining prepayment), with a copy button per value for "Mein ELSTER" (bases in whole euros, taxes with a decimal comma), the due date (the 10th after the period, one month later with permanent extension, a weekend moves to the Monday) and a CSV download. The accrual basis counts an invoice when its service ends and a cancellation on its own date; the cash basis counts payments, in proportion for part payments. A small business sees that no advance return is needed. The view says that it is an estimate and not tax advice, lists what is not covered (for example § 13b UStG received, other rates, the special prepayment) and reports expenses and invoices it cannot place. The field numbers and the due date rules are in the tax parameters of 2026 and 2027 with their sources.
- Reports (F11), a new area "Auswertungen" in the sidebar: the income of a calendar year per customer and month (the net part of the payments, on the day they arrived), the expenses per category and month (without the VAT that comes back), the open items (issued invoices with the open amount, due date and days overdue, and sums per customer, from all years) and the year against the year before, month by month. A year selector, accessible tables, and every report as a CSV file (the same encoder as the list exports, so text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` stays text). All figures are labelled as estimates. A search over contacts, items, quotes, invoices (also by number) and expenses lists the hits with the kind of record; it does not jump to the record yet.
- Overview of the calendar year (Phase 4, "Wo stehe ich?"): the net income from the payments received, the net expenses (without the VAT that comes back), an estimate of the surplus on the cash basis, what is still open on issued invoices and what of it is overdue, the twelve months, and the turnover against the small business limits (25,000 € of the previous year, 100,000 € of the current year, § 19 Abs. 1 UStG, kept in the tax parameters with their source). Every figure is an estimate for orientation.
- Exit at any time (F12): Settings, App, "Ordner als ZIP exportieren" downloads a ZIP file of the company folder as it is, with every list as a CSV file under `listen/` and a readme. Nothing in the folder changes; unsaved changes are written first. The format is described in `docs/data-format.md`.
- Receipts of expenses (Phase 3): a PDF, PNG or JPEG of up to 10 MB is copied into the company folder under `belege/<year>/`, named after the expense and never overwritten, or a https link to the place where the receipt is kept is saved. The receipt can be downloaded (its checksum is checked first), replaced or removed; the file of a removed receipt stays in the folder. Every change is written to the audit log.
- Import of contacts and items (F36) from a CSV or an Excel file. The columns are matched to the fields, with the matches suggested from the header line; the decimal style of the prices is chosen. Each row is checked like a typed one, and the preview shows which rows are fine and why the others are not. Only the rows that are fine are imported, as one batch that can be taken back as a whole; the file itself is not kept. A row without a customer or item number gets the next free one of the pattern. A CSV file is read in UTF-8 or Windows-1252 and may be at most 5 MB with 5,000 rows.
- Excel export (F37): every list can be exported as an `.xlsx` file next to the CSV file, with amounts as numbers and dates as dates. Excel files are read and written without a library; the app reads the ZIP archive and the XML itself, with limits on size, number of parts and file names.
- ADR 0006, proposed: how an AI agent on the desktop gets access to the company folder (F38). Nothing of it is built yet.
- CSV export of the lists (F35): contacts, items, quotes, invoices and expenses, each as a file for a spreadsheet. The file has a header line, `;` as the separator and a byte order mark, so that umlauts show up in Excel; amounts have a decimal comma (`1250,00`), rates are plain numbers (`19`), dates are ISO dates. A text that a spreadsheet could read as a formula (for example `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` at the start) is written with a leading apostrophe. The file is downloaded; the company folder does not change. Payments have no list of their own yet.
- Expenses (first part of Phase 3): what the business paid for, with date, supplier, receipt number, gross amount, the VAT as the receipt shows it (German VAT at 19 %, 7 % or 0 %, reverse charge, a supplier outside the EU, or no VAT), category, the account it was paid from and a note. The list shows the newest first with the VAT inside each amount and searches by supplier, receipt number, category and note. A removed expense stays in its file. Receipt files follow.
- Decisions of the customer on a finalized quote (F21): a quote is accepted or declined, with its date, on its page. Each decision is a file of its own; the quote does not change. A wrong decision is removed, and both are logged. The list shows whether a quote is open, accepted or declined, and filters by it.
- Setup assistant for the first start and for "Add company": browser check, folder, company details with logo (PNG, JPG or SVG up to 1 MB), tax settings (small business scheme, cash or accrual VAT, return period, permanent extension, freelance or trade with multiplier), bank account with IBAN check, invoice number format with preview, payment term and footer, and a summary. Every step is checked; nothing is written before "Finish".
- Several companies, each in its own folder. A switcher in the sidebar lists them and adds new ones; the last opened company opens again after a restart.
- Settings page with the fields of the assistant, saved section by section.
- App frame with the planned areas (dashboard, quotes, invoices, expenses, contacts, taxes, tools) shown as coming soon.
- Daily backup of the company file in the company folder (14 days kept).
- German and English interface.
- The tax step of the setup assistant and the tax settings say who kontor is for (sole proprietors with cash-basis accounting, not corporations or partnerships) and that several businesses of one person share one VAT status.
- For a trade, the tax step of the setup assistant and the tax settings link to the map of the statistical offices with the trade tax multipliers of all municipalities (opens in a new window). The municipality's own statute stays binding.
- Contacts: customers and suppliers, as company or private person, with address, invoice email, customer number (unique), EU VAT ID, Leitweg-ID for public buyers, default order or PO number, payment term and notes. Search and filter by role; deleting asks first and keeps the file, so documents that use the contact are not affected. A contact file that cannot be read is reported, the others still load. Contacts are part of the daily backup.
- Bank accounts: a company can have several accounts, bank accounts with IBAN and PayPal, managed in the settings. One of them is the default for invoices and cannot be removed; another account can be made the default. The same IBAN or PayPal address cannot be added twice. A removed account stays in the company file, so documents and payments that use it are not affected.
- Catalogue of items and services: number, name, description, unit, net price and VAT rate (19 %, 7 % or 0 %), with search, edit and delete (the file stays). Prices are typed and shown in the interface language; a small business sees prices without VAT. The units are the codes e-invoices use. Items are part of the daily backup and of the emergency copy.
- Quotes as drafts: customer, date, valid until (30 days by default), subject, introduction and closing text. Lines come from the catalogue or are typed freely, with quantity (up to three decimal places, negative for a deduction), unit, unit price and VAT rate; they can be moved and removed. A deduction can lower the lines of its VAT rate, but not below zero; the check shows while typing. Totals show the VAT per rate, rounded once per rate; a small business sees the total and the note on § 19 UStG. The list shows the amount, marks expired quotes and filters them by validity (all, valid, expired, with counts).
- Invoices as drafts: customer, invoice date, date or period of service, subject, order or PO number and Leitweg-ID (taken over from the customer), introduction and closing text, and the lines as in quotes. The payment term comes from the customer or the company and shows the due date; the account the invoice is paid to is the default account unless another one is chosen. With regular VAT the invoice is either with VAT, reverse charge (§ 13b UStG, "Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers"), a tax-free intra-community supply or a service not taxable in Germany (place of supply abroad, § 3a UStG, for example for a customer outside the EU); without VAT the totals show the note the invoice needs. A hint appears when the choice does not fit where the customer is. A draft can be saved incomplete: the form lists what is still missing before it can be issued (a customer, a line, the customer's address except for small amount invoices up to 250 € gross, both VAT IDs where § 14a UStG requires them, an account that was not removed). The list shows the drafts with their due date and amount. Invoice numbers, the PDF and issuing follow.
- PDF of quotes and invoice drafts, downloaded from their form: a business letter after DIN 5008 (address field for window envelopes, information block with dates, numbers and references, fold marks) with the logo, the lines over as many pages as needed with the table header repeated, totals per VAT rate, the note on VAT with both VAT IDs where required, the payment request with due date and account, or the validity of a quote, and a footer with address, tax number and bank details on every page. Drafts say "ENTWURF" across the page and have no number yet. Changes not saved yet are saved first. The font (Inter) is embedded; characters it does not have are reported. An SVG logo is drawn as an image.
- Issuing invoices (ADR 0004): a complete draft gets the next number of the pattern in the settings, counted per year of the invoice date when the pattern contains a year. The number is reserved in `numbering/invoice.json` and read back before anything else is written; the PDF is written once to `documents/issued/<number>.pdf`, its SHA-256 and a snapshot of the company, customer and account details are kept in the invoice, and an entry is appended to `audit/<YYYY-MM>.jsonl`. Issuing asks first and saves changes not saved yet. An issued invoice can be neither changed nor deleted, not even by a change from another device; its PDF is the stored file, and a warning appears when the file no longer matches its SHA-256. A number that exists already stops issuing instead of being given twice; a second attempt after an interruption goes on with the same number. The list shows the numbers and filters drafts and issued invoices.
- Cancelling issued invoices: "Cancel invoice" asks first, then issues a cancellation invoice (Stornorechnung) with the next number of the invoice range, dated today but never before the invoice. It has the invoice's lines with the opposite quantity, so every amount and the VAT per rate are reversed exactly, keeps the invoice's company, customer and account details and VAT treatment, and names the invoice it cancels with number and date (§ 31 Abs. 5 UStDV); its PDF says "Stornorechnung" and has no payment request. Like issuing, it writes the PDF once with its SHA-256 and appends an audit entry (`cancel`); a second attempt after an interruption goes on with the same number, and an invoice is cancelled only once. The invoice itself does not change; it is shown as cancelled and no longer due, with a link to its cancellation and back. "Copy as new invoice" starts a draft with everything from an issued invoice, dated today, for the corrected invoice.
- Finalizing quotes ("Angebot abschließen"): a quote with a customer and at least one line that has not run out gets the next number of the quote format in the settings (`AN-{YYYY}-{NNNN}` by default, set in the setup assistant and the invoice settings, with a preview; it must differ from the invoice format). Quotes are counted on their own, per year of the quote date, and need no gapless numbers. The number is reserved in `numbering/quote.json`, the PDF is written once to `documents/quotes/<number>.pdf` without the draft marks, its SHA-256 and a snapshot of the company, customer and account details are kept in the quote, and an entry is appended to `audit/<YYYY-MM>.jsonl` (`issueQuote`). Finalizing asks first and saves changes not saved yet; the form lists what is still missing. A finalized quote can be neither changed nor deleted, also not by a change from another device; its PDF is the stored file. It can still become an invoice, which names the quote number, and "Copy as new quote" starts a draft with everything from it, dated today. The list shows the quote numbers and finds quotes by them.
- Quote versions: "New version" on a finalized quote starts a draft with everything from it, dated today, that replaces it. Finalizing gives the version the number of the first version with a suffix (`AN-2026-0001-2`, `-3`, …), without a new number of the range, and its PDF says which quote it replaces (number and date). A quote has one version at a time; a draft whose quote got another finalized version cannot be finalized. The replaced quote stays as it was and is marked as replaced, with links between the versions. Every finalized quote lists its versions with their totals and what changed from one to the next (lines added or removed, quantity, unit, price, VAT rate, description), and how often the customer's quotes were changed; the list counts finalized quotes, those changed and the changes in all.
- "Create invoice" in a saved quote opens a new invoice with the quote's customer, subject and lines; changes to the quote that are not saved yet are saved first.
- Dunning (Mahnungen) of overdue invoices: the payment reminder and the dunning levels 1 to 3. Each claims the default interest since the due date (for a business customer) or since the first reminder (for a consumer), at the base rate of each half year plus 9 points (business) or 5 points (consumer). A business customer is charged the flat fee of 40 € once for the claim, and a dunning fee can be typed in. The form shows what the dunning claims before it is saved. A dunning is kept as it was issued and recorded in the audit log. The letter as a PDF and its sending follow.
- Customer account per customer, opened from the contact: the invoices, cancellations and payments in date order, with the balance after each entry and the amount overdue. A payment on an invoice that was cancelled later shows as a credit for the customer to be refunded.
- Payments of issued invoices: a payment records the date the money arrived, the amount and the bank account it arrived on, and a note. Part payments are possible; the invoice shows what is paid and what is still open. The list shows whether an invoice is partly paid, paid or overdue, and filters for open and overdue invoices. A payment is never changed: a wrong one is removed (soft delete), and both are logged in the audit log. A payment above the open amount is refused. Payments are part of the daily backup.
- Sending by e-mail (F04): an issued invoice, its cancellation, a finalized quote or a dunning can be sent as an e-mail file (.eml) with its PDF attached, which opens as a draft in Outlook or Thunderbird. Or the mail program opens with the recipient, the subject and the text, and the PDF is downloaded to be attached. The text is filled in from the document and can be changed in the form; kontor sends nothing itself, and each prepared mail is written to the audit log. A PDF that changed since it was issued is not sent.
- The letter of a dunning is a PDF: the claim with its open amount, the default interest of each dunning, the dunning fee and the flat fee, the date to pay by and the interest rate it was calculated with. It is made from the dunning and the invoice as issued.

- Discounts (F23) on quotes and invoices: a line takes off a percentage or an amount, and the whole document does too. The VAT is calculated on the discounted amounts per tax rate, as before. The PDF shows the lines before the discount, the discount and then the net amount, and a discounted line says what it took off. A discount larger than its line or than the document is refused, and a cancellation reverses the discounts of its invoice exactly. The version history shows a changed discount. Finalized quotes and issued invoices keep the discounts they were issued with.

- Documents in English for English customers (F05): a contact has a language for its documents, German or English (German by default). Invoices, quotes and dunnings, their PDFs and their e-mails are made in the language of the customer. The language of an issued invoice or quote is fixed in its PDF when it is issued.

- Automatic numbers for contacts and items (F28): a new customer or supplier starts with the next free number of the pattern in the settings (`K-{NNNN}` by default), and a new item with the next free item number (`A-{NNNN}`). The number is shown in the form and can be changed there. A deleted contact or item keeps its number, so no number is given twice. The patterns are set in the invoice settings with a preview, and they may not contain a date.

- Small business (§ 19 UStG): an invoice can be for a service whose place of supply is abroad (§ 3a UStG). It shows no VAT and the note of § 3a instead of the note of § 19 UStG. Every other choice of a small business stays "no VAT, § 19".

### Fixed

- Dunning interest: the base rate table (§ 247 BGB) now ends with the half year of its last entry (31 December 2026 for the rate of 1 July 2026). Interest over later days is refused as "no base rate known for this period" instead of being counted silently with the last rate, which would have put a wrong amount into a dunning that cannot be changed. The table has to be extended with each Bundesbank announcement. The values up to 1 July 2026 (1,52 %) were checked against the Bundesbank's table on 7 November 2026.
- Timestamps read from the company folder must be in the canonical form `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ` (what the app writes) and at most 366 days ahead of the clock of the device. Before, any string that `Date.parse` understood was accepted (for example `Tue Jan 01 3000`), and one file with the year 9999 moved the clock of every later edit of the session to that year, so that those edits won every field merge. A timestamp further ahead is refused with a message naming the field, and is never used to move the clock. A device whose clock runs ahead by up to a year stays readable.
- Saving to the company folder: a file that another device or a sync client replaced is now noticed and merged whenever its modification time or size differs from what this app last read or wrote, not only when its time is newer. Before, a replaced file with an older time (a device whose clock is behind, or a sync client that keeps the source time) was overwritten without a merge, which could drop an issued invoice from its record.
- Issuing, cancelling and finalizing a quote: a PDF left in `documents/` by an interrupted attempt is now recorded only if it is byte for byte what the document gives at the retry (the PDF is made with the time of the reservation as its creation date, so it is reproducible). If the draft was edited in between, or the file is not ours, nothing is issued, the file is not overwritten, and a message explains how to go on. Before, the old PDF was recorded with its hash for the edited draft, so record and PDF disagreed.
- Issuing and cancelling: a number reserved by an interrupted attempt is used again only while it still fits the document. If the invoice date moved into another year (or another month in a pattern with `{MM}`) before the retry, or a cancellation is retried in the next year, the document gets a new number of its year; the old reservation stays in the range file as a gap. Before, the number kept the old year although the counter restarts by the invoice date (ADR 0004).
- Part payments no longer add or lose a cent of income: the overview and the report "income per customer" took the net part of every payment rounded on its own, so an invoice paid in several parts could show 1 cent more or less than its net amount (for example 10.01 € instead of 10.00 €). Now the net part comes from the cumulative amount paid per invoice, in the order of the payments, as in the advance VAT return, so a fully paid invoice shows exactly its net amount and the three places agree. A payment above the gross amount adds nothing to the net income (#68).
- A change of the VAT status no longer changes the expenses of earlier years: the overview, the report "expenses per category" and the year comparison counted every expense with the status of the company today, so switching from regular VAT to small business (or back) turned the expenses of past years gross (or net). An expense now counts with the status valid on its day, taken from the issued invoices around it (each carries the company as it was when issued). The company file has no history of the status, so there is no exact change date: a day after the last invoice counts with that invoice's status in the same calendar year and with the status of the company from the next year on, and the hint of the report says so (#70).
- Export of the folder: a file whose name holds characters that some programs read as a path when unpacking (`\`, `:`, `..`, control characters, a leading or trailing space) is left out of the archive, named in `LIESMICH.txt`, and the export says how many. Before, such a name from another system went into the archive unchecked, and unpacking it on Windows could write outside the target folder. The files are now read one at a time while the archive is written, instead of the whole folder first, and a folder of more than 65,000 files or 2 GB is refused with a message before anything is read, instead of a ZIP file that is too large for the format (#80).
- The logo file in the folder is checked before it is shown or printed: its size before it is read, then its SHA-256 and its type against `company.json`, and its size in pixels from the header of the PNG or JPEG (at most 10,000 pixels a side and 25 megapixels). Before, any file under the logo's name was used: a replaced logo in a shared folder was printed on invoices without notice, and a small file declaring a huge picture could stop issuing after the number was reserved. A logo that does not pass is neither shown nor printed; the logo settings say why, and the confirmation before issuing, cancelling or finalizing says that the document is made without it. An upload with more pixels than that is refused the same way (#78).
- Advance VAT return on the cash basis: intra-community supplies (Kz 41) now count in the period of the invoice, at the latest in the one in which the month after the supply ends, and services to businesses in other EU states (Kz 21) in the period of the service, as § 18b UStG says and as in the summary report; a cancellation of them counts negative on its own date. Before, both followed the payment, so an EU invoice of March paid in April was declared in the second quarter, and an unpaid one never; the comparison of the summary report with Kz 21 showed a difference for every unpaid invoice. On the accrual basis an intra-community supply invoiced after the month of the supply now also counts by the invoice. Kz 60 (§ 13b Abs. 5 UStG) and Kz 45 still follow the payment on the cash basis; that rule could not be verified, and the list of what is not covered says so. The wording of § 18b and § 17 was read at dejure.org; the instructions of the form (USt 1 E) could not be read (#75).
- The emergency copy of unsaved changes in the browser is signed with a random key kept in the company folder (`.kontor/emergency-copy.key`). Every page opened from disk shares the browser's storage, so another local HTML file could write a copy that kontor took over at the next start, an issued invoice included. Now a copy without the folder's signature is removed and reported, and even a signed copy never brings back issued documents, payments, dunnings or decisions on quotes, which are in the folder already. Unsigned copies of earlier versions are removed as well. SECURITY.md and the README describe the shared storage and what it still allows (#69).
- A company is open in one window of the browser at a time. A second window or tab (or a second double click on `index.html`) shows that the company is open elsewhere, instead of opening it with its own copy of the state; "Open here" takes it over, and the first window saves what is pending, closes the company and says that it was opened elsewhere. Before, two windows did not see each other's changes and could give the same invoice number twice. A second device is still not detected (#97).
- Files of the folder that cannot be read when a company is opened (a file being synchronised, written by a newer kontor, or damaged) are now listed with their name and the reason on the overview, the reports and the taxes for as long as the company is open, with the note that the figures may be incomplete. Before, each kind of record had a toast of four seconds without file names, and the toasts of one load replaced each other, so an invoice could be missing from the advance VAT return without a visible cause. A load shows one toast. While files cannot be read, no daily backup is written, so that the older, complete backups are not pushed out of the 14 that are kept (#96).
- Saving: a change the folder refuses for good (the file was written by a newer kontor, is damaged or not kontor's, or conflicts with a document issued elsewhere) no longer blocks the work. Before, one such record kept the save status at "error" for the whole session, so issuing, payments, dunnings, quote decisions, sending by e-mail and the export of the folder were all refused. Now the other records are still written, the sidebar lists the refused change with its reason, and "Discard" drops it and reads the company from the folder again; "Try again" tries it once more. The export of the folder offers to export the folder as it is, without the unsaved changes. When the browser cannot keep the emergency copy of unsaved changes (its storage of about 5 MB is full), the sidebar now warns not to close the window; before, the copy was dropped without a word (#95).
- Saving: "Try again" in the sidebar asks for the access to the folder again when the folder refused it, so a permission that was revoked or ran out during the work can be renewed without reloading the page. A file that another program holds (Excel, a virus scanner, the sync client) has its own message, and such a failure, like an unexpected one, is tried again by itself after 2 s, 10 s, 30 s, 1 and 2 minutes; before, it stayed unsaved until the next click or change (#94).
- A link to `index.html?e2e` no longer switches the app into the test mode of the browser tests, which kept the company in `localStorage` (readable by every local HTML file) instead of the chosen folder, without asking for a folder. The test mode is now switched on only by the test setup itself, before the app starts (#83).
- The tabs of the reports work with the keyboard as screen readers announce them: the arrow keys move to the next or previous report (and wrap around), Home and End to the first and the last, and only the selected tab is in the Tab order. Before, they were announced as tabs but behaved as plain buttons (#82).
- Payments can no longer be dated after today. A typo in the year (2027 instead of 2026) booked the income in the later year, in the overview, the reports and the advance VAT return, while the invoice already showed as paid; the payment could only be removed and typed again. The form and the recording both refuse such a date. A date before the invoice date is still accepted (a prepayment), and the form points it out below the field (#79).
- Issuing an invoice or a cancellation, finalizing a quote and starting a new quote version no longer get slower with every PDF in `documents/issued/` or `documents/quotes/`: listing the existing numbers opened every PDF to read its size and date, which were not used (about 2.4 s per click with 2,000 issued invoices). Now only the names are listed (#88).
- The overview (Home), the reports, the advance VAT return, the customer account and the invoice list export no longer freeze with thousands of invoices: "is this invoice cancelled?" and "what was paid on it?" were looked up by scanning every document and every payment once per invoice, so the time grew with the square of the data (22 s for the overview at 20,000 invoices). They are looked up in an index built once per computation (about 0.2 s at 20,000 invoices); the figures are unchanged (#85).
- The hint of the footer field says that address, contact details, tax number and bank details are printed in the footer already, so they are not typed a second time.
- The window showed a second vertical and a horizontal scroll bar: the hidden file field of the logo stuck out of the content area. Now only the content area scrolls.
