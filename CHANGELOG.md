# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Decisions of the customer on a finalized quote (F21): a quote is accepted or declined, with its date, on its page. Each decision is a file of its own; the quote does not change. A wrong decision is removed, and both are logged. The list shows whether a quote is open, accepted or declined, and filters by it.
- Setup assistant for the first start and for "Add company": browser check, folder, company details with logo (PNG, JPG or SVG up to 1 MB), tax settings (small business scheme, cash or accrual VAT, return period, permanent extension, freelance or trade with multiplier), bank account with IBAN check, invoice number format with preview, payment term and footer, and a summary. Every step is checked; nothing is written before "Finish".
- Several companies, each in its own folder. A switcher in the sidebar lists them and adds new ones; the last opened company opens again after a restart.
- Settings page with the fields of the assistant, saved section by section.
- App frame with the planned areas (dashboard, quotes, invoices, expenses, contacts, taxes, tools) shown as coming soon.
- Daily backup of the company file in the company folder (14 days kept).
- German and English interface.
- The tax step of the setup assistant and the tax settings say who kontor is for (sole proprietors with cash-basis accounting, not corporations or partnerships) and that several businesses of one person share one VAT status.
- For a trade, the tax step of the setup assistant and the tax settings link to the map of the statistical offices with the trade tax multipliers of all municipalities (opens in a new window). The municipality's own statute stays binding.
- Contacts: customers and suppliers, as company or private person, with address, invoice email, customer number (unique), EU VAT ID, Leitweg-ID for public buyers, default order or PO number, payment term and notes. Search and filter by role; deleting asks first and keeps the file, so documents that use the contact are not affected. A contact file that cannot be read is reported, the others still load. Contacts are part of the daily backup.
- Bank accounts: a company can have several accounts, bank accounts with IBAN and PayPal, managed in the settings. One of them is the default for invoices and cannot be removed; another account can be made the default. The same IBAN or PayPal address cannot be added twice. A removed account stays in the company file, so documents and payments that use it are not affected.
- Catalogue of items and services: number, name, description, unit, net price and VAT rate (19 %, 7 % or 0 %), with search, edit and delete (the file stays). Prices are typed and shown in the interface language; a small business sees prices without VAT. The units are the codes e-invoices use. Items are part of the daily backup and of the emergency copy.
- Quotes as drafts: customer, date, valid until (30 days by default), subject, introduction and closing text. Lines come from the catalogue or are typed freely, with quantity (up to three decimal places, negative for a deduction), unit, unit price and VAT rate; they can be moved and removed. A deduction can lower the lines of its VAT rate, but not below zero; the check shows while typing. Totals show the VAT per rate, rounded once per rate; a small business sees the total and the note on § 19 UStG. The list shows the amount, marks expired quotes and filters them by validity (all, valid, expired, with counts).
- Invoices as drafts: customer, invoice date, date or period of service, subject, order or PO number and Leitweg-ID (taken over from the customer), introduction and closing text, and the lines as in quotes. The payment term comes from the customer or the company and shows the due date; the account the invoice is paid to is the default account unless another one is chosen. With regular VAT the invoice is either with VAT, reverse charge (§ 13b UStG, "Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers"), a tax-free intra-community supply or a service not taxable in Germany (place of supply abroad, § 3a UStG, for example for a customer outside the EU); without VAT the totals show the note the invoice needs. A hint appears when the choice does not fit where the customer is. A draft can be saved incomplete: the form lists what is still missing before it can be issued (a customer, a line, the customer's address except for small amount invoices up to 250 € gross, both VAT IDs where § 14a UStG requires them, an account that was not removed). The list shows the drafts with their due date and amount. Invoice numbers, the PDF and issuing follow.
- PDF of quotes and invoice drafts, downloaded from their form: a business letter after DIN 5008 (address field for window envelopes, information block with dates, numbers and references, fold marks) with the logo, the lines over as many pages as needed with the table header repeated, totals per VAT rate, the note on VAT with both VAT IDs where required, the payment request with due date and account, or the validity of a quote, and a footer with address, tax number and bank details on every page. Drafts say "ENTWURF" across the page and have no number yet. Changes not saved yet are saved first. The font (Inter) is embedded; characters it does not have are reported. An SVG logo is drawn as an image.
- Issuing invoices (ADR 0004): a complete draft gets the next number of the pattern in the settings, counted per year of the invoice date when the pattern contains a year. The number is reserved in `numbering/invoice.json` and read back before anything else is written; the PDF is written once to `documents/issued/<number>.pdf`, its SHA-256 and a snapshot of the company, customer and account details are kept in the invoice, and an entry is appended to `audit/<YYYY-MM>.jsonl`. Issuing asks first and saves changes not saved yet. An issued invoice can be neither changed nor deleted, not even by a change from another device; its PDF is the stored file, and a warning appears when the file no longer matches its SHA-256. A number that exists already stops issuing instead of being given twice; a second attempt after an interruption goes on with the same number. The list shows the numbers and filters drafts and issued invoices.
- Cancelling issued invoices: "Cancel invoice" asks first, then issues a cancellation invoice (Stornorechnung) with the next number of the invoice range, dated today but never before the invoice. It has the invoice's lines with the opposite quantity, so every amount and the VAT per rate are reversed exactly, keeps the invoice's company, customer and account details and VAT treatment, and names the invoice it cancels with number and date (§ 31 Abs. 5 UStDV); its PDF says "Stornorechnung" and has no payment request. Like issuing, it writes the PDF once with its SHA-256 and appends an audit entry (`cancel`); a second attempt after an interruption goes on with the same number, and an invoice is cancelled only once. The invoice itself does not change; it is shown as cancelled and no longer due, with a link to its cancellation and back. "Copy as new invoice" starts a draft with everything from an issued invoice, dated today, for the corrected invoice.
- Finalizing quotes ("Angebot abschließen"): a quote with a customer and at least one line that has not run out gets the next number of the quote format in the settings (`AN-{YYYY}-{NNNN}` by default, set in the setup assistant and the invoice settings, with a preview; it must differ from the invoice format). Quotes are counted on their own, per year of the quote date, and need no gapless numbers. The number is reserved in `numbering/quote.json`, the PDF is written once to `documents/quotes/<number>.pdf` without the draft marks, its SHA-256 and a snapshot of the company, customer and account details are kept in the quote, and an entry is appended to `audit/<YYYY-MM>.jsonl` (`issueQuote`). Finalizing asks first and saves changes not saved yet; the form lists what is still missing. A finalized quote can be neither changed nor deleted, also not by a change from another device; its PDF is the stored file. It can still become an invoice, which names the quote number, and "Copy as new quote" starts a draft with everything from it, dated today and with no customer chosen yet. The list shows the quote numbers and finds quotes by them.
- Quote versions: "New version" on a finalized quote starts a draft with everything from it, dated today, that replaces it. Finalizing gives the version the number of the first version with a suffix (`AN-2026-0001-2`, `-3`, …), without a new number of the range, and its PDF says which quote it replaces (number and date). A quote has one version at a time; a draft whose quote got another finalized version cannot be finalized. The replaced quote stays as it was and is marked as replaced, with links between the versions. Every finalized quote lists its versions with their totals and what changed from one to the next (lines added or removed, quantity, unit, price, VAT rate, description), and how often the customer's quotes were changed; the list counts finalized quotes, those changed and the changes in all.
- "Create invoice" in a saved quote opens a new invoice with the quote's customer, subject and lines; changes to the quote that are not saved yet are saved first.
- Dunning (Mahnungen) of overdue invoices: the payment reminder and the dunning levels 1 to 3. Each claims the default interest since the due date (for a business customer) or since the first reminder (for a consumer), at the base rate of each half year plus 9 points (business) or 5 points (consumer). A business customer is charged the flat fee of 40 € once for the claim, and a dunning fee can be typed in. The form shows what the dunning claims before it is saved. A dunning is kept as it was issued and recorded in the audit log. The letter as a PDF and its sending follow.
- Customer account per customer, opened from the contact: the invoices, cancellations and payments in date order, with the balance after each entry and the amount overdue. A payment on an invoice that was cancelled later shows as a credit for the customer to be refunded.
- Payments of issued invoices: a payment records the date the money arrived, the amount and the bank account it arrived on, and a note. Part payments are possible; the invoice shows what is paid and what is still open. The list shows whether an invoice is partly paid, paid or overdue, and filters for open and overdue invoices. A payment is never changed: a wrong one is removed (soft delete), and both are logged in the audit log. A payment above the open amount is refused. Payments are part of the daily backup.
- Sending by e-mail (F04): an issued invoice, its cancellation, a finalized quote or a dunning can be sent as an e-mail file (.eml) with its PDF attached, which opens as a draft in Outlook or Thunderbird. Or the mail program opens with the recipient, the subject and the text, and the PDF is downloaded to be attached. The text is filled in from the document and can be changed in the form; kontor sends nothing itself, and each prepared mail is written to the audit log. A PDF that changed since it was issued is not sent.
- The letter of a dunning is a PDF: the claim with its open amount, the default interest of each dunning, the dunning fee and the flat fee, the date to pay by and the interest rate it was calculated with. It is made from the dunning and the invoice as issued.

- Discounts (F23) on quotes and invoices: a line takes off a percentage or an amount, and the whole document does too. The VAT is calculated on the discounted amounts per tax rate, as before. The PDF shows the lines before the discount, the discount and then the net amount, and a discounted line says what it took off. A discount larger than its line or than the document is refused, and a cancellation reverses the discounts of its invoice exactly. The version history shows a changed discount. Finalized quotes and issued invoices keep the discounts they were issued with.

- Documents in English for English customers (F05): a contact has a language for its documents, German or English (German by default). Invoices, quotes and dunnings, their PDFs and their e-mails are made in the language of the customer. The language of an issued invoice or quote is fixed in its PDF when it is issued.

- Automatic numbers for contacts and items (F28): a new customer or supplier starts with the next free number of the pattern in the settings (`K-{NNNN}` by default), and a new item with the next free item number (`A-{NNNN}`). The number is shown in the form and can be changed there. A deleted contact or item keeps its number, so no number is given twice. The patterns are set in the invoice settings with a preview, and they may not contain a date.

### Changed

- "Als neues Angebot kopieren" starts a draft with the lines, texts and discount of the finalized quote, dated and valid from today, but with no customer: the customer is chosen anew, and the copy is a quote of its own (no version of the original). A new version of a quote keeps its customer.
- For a private person the contact form hides the VAT ID, the Leitweg-ID, the order number and the contact person; the section with the payment term and the notes is called "Payment and notes". What is typed for a private person in these fields is not saved, and the form says so.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 12: quotes and invoices have discounts on their lines and on the whole document; a document of version 11 has none. No other file changes.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 11: decisions of the customer on a quote are a new kind of file (`quoteStatus/`); no other file changes.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 10: the settings have the patterns of contact and item numbers (defaults `K-{NNNN}` and `A-{NNNN}`); no other file changes.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 9: a contact has the language of its documents (German for existing contacts), and the customer in an issued document gets it too; no other file changes.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 8: dunnings are a new kind of file (`dunnings/`); no existing file changes.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 7: payments are a new kind of file (`payments/`); no existing file changes.
- The trade tax multiplier must be at least 280 % from 2027 (§ 16 Abs. 4 Satz 2 GewStG; 200 % up to 2026). The forms check against the minimum of the current year; in 2026 they say that a lower multiplier is replaced by 280 % from 2027. Company folders with a lower multiplier from an earlier year still open.
- All files of a company folder have schema version 6: invoices are a new kind of document (version 3), can be issued (version 4) and cancelled (version 5), and quotes can be finalized and get new versions (version 6; `company.json` gets the quote format `AN-{YYYY}-{NNNN}`). A folder is upgraded when it is read; older versions of kontor then report that the folder was saved by a newer version.
- `company.json` has schema version 2: the one bank account becomes a list of accounts with a default. A company folder is upgraded when it is read and saved in the new format with the next change; older versions of kontor then report that the folder was saved by a newer version.
- The content area is a panel with rounded corners on a slightly darker frame, and cards and lists are white on a light grey background, so they stand out better (light and dark mode).
- Settings: a side panel on the right lists the sections (company details, tax, invoices, bank accounts, logo, app) and shows one at a time. Changes that are not saved yet stay when switching sections, and the side panel marks the section.
- The forms for contacts, items and quotes have the same width. Contacts and quotes have a side panel on the right with jump marks to their sections; it stays in view while scrolling and marks the section being read. On a narrower window the lines of a quote use two rows.

### Fixed

- The hint of the footer field says that address, contact details, tax number and bank details are printed in the footer already, so they are not typed a second time.
- The window showed a second vertical and a horizontal scroll bar: the hidden file field of the logo stuck out of the content area. Now only the content area scrolls.
